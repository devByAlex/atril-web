01Who we are

Atril is an app for musicians and bands, built by Alex Ordoñez ("we" or "Atril"). This policy explains what information we collect when you use the app, what we use it for and what control you have over it.

02What we collect

Account data. When you sign up we store your email address and the name you choose to show in the app, along with the language you use Atril in, so we can write to you in it.

If you sign in with Apple or Google, they give us your email and, the first time only, your name; never your password. If you choose "Hide My Email", Apple gives us an address that forwards to yours and we never see the real one. When you delete your account we ask Apple to revoke the permission you gave Atril.

Content you create. The songs you add (title, artist, lyrics, chords, key, BPM, YouTube or Spotify links) and the setlists you build (name, date, song order and the length of each song).

Collaboration data. If you share a setlist with other musicians using an invitation code, they can see your name, the messages you write in that setlist's chat, your annotations (highlights or notes on the lyrics) and the key you pick for each song — and you see the same from them.

Automatic technical data. Standard connection information (such as your IP address) collected by our infrastructure provider, and whether you are connected in real time during rehearsal mode, visible only to the other members of that setlist.

We use no third-party analytics or advertising tools. We collect no more data than the app needs to work.

03How we use your data

  • To create and manage your account and keep you signed in.
  • To store your songs and setlists and sync them across your devices.
  • To run the real-time features: rehearsal mode, chat and shared annotations.
  • To contact you when needed (for example, about a problem with your account).

We never use your data for advertising and we never sell it to anyone.

04Who we share it with

Supabase, our database, authentication and real-time infrastructure provider, hosts all of the data described above and acts as a data processor on our behalf.

Other members of a shared setlist see the collaboration information described above (your name, chat messages, annotations and key) — never your email or any other account data.

Apple and Google, if you sign in with them: your device talks to them directly and only the data described above reaches us.

Our email provider, which delivers account confirmation and password reset messages. It only receives your email address and the text of that message.

Payment processors. Subscriptions are billed directly by the Apple App Store, Google Play and RevenueCat. Atril never stores or has access to your card details.

05How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the app itself, under Profile → Delete my account: your profile, songs and setlists are permanently deleted, along with your chat messages, annotations and custom keys. It is immediate and irreversible, and you don't need to write to us.

Note that deleting your account also deletes the setlists you shared, so the musicians who joined them will lose access.

06Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, request a copy (portability) or ask us to delete it. If you live in the European Union, these rights are granted by the GDPR. To exercise any of them, email us.

07Security

Your session is protected by encrypted authentication, and data access is controlled row by row (Row Level Security): each user can only see and change the information they are allowed to, whether their own or from a setlist shared with them.

08Children

Atril is not intended for children under 13 without adult supervision. If your group or band includes minors, the account must be managed or supervised by a responsible adult.

09Changes to this policy

If we make a significant change to this policy, we will let you know inside the app. The "last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the current version.

10Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, email us at filipth24@gmail.com.
