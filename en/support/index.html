Support

We reply within 24-48 hours

Something not working, a question, or something you wish Atril did? Write to filipth24@gmail.com and you'll get an answer. Atril is made by one person, so every message gets read.

How to get help

Email filipth24@gmail.com and tell us what's going on. If it's a bug, it helps a lot to include:

  • Which device you use (iPhone or iPad) and which iOS version.
  • What you were doing when it happened.
  • A screenshot, if you can.

You can also write to suggest a feature. Atril has been built on what working musicians asked for.

I can't sign in or I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot your password? and you'll get a link to set a new one. If it doesn't show up within a few minutes, check your spam folder. If it still doesn't arrive, write to us and we'll sort it out by hand.

Inviting my band to a setlist

Open the setlist, go to its options and generate the invite code: six characters. Each musician enters it from their own phone or tablet and joins the setlist. From then on everyone sees the same songs, each with their own key and capo, and can use the chat, the notes and rehearsal mode.

Adding songs

You can type them, paste the text with the chords, or paste the address of the chord site you already use. Atril detects the chords and places them over the lyrics. If the page has no chords, only the lyrics are imported.

Key and capo

The key changes with one tap, and everyone keeps their own without touching anyone else's. With a capo, Atril shows the chords that actually sound, so the whole band reads the same thing. If you're the guitarist and want to read your own shapes, leave the capo empty.

Trial and subscription

Atril is free for one month, everything included. After that, the subscription is 3.99 EUR per month or 34.99 EUR per year, renews automatically and can be cancelled at any time.

How to cancel the subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, so you cancel from your device: Settings → your name at the top → Subscriptions → Atril → Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the period you already paid for ends.

How to delete my account

From inside the app: Profile → Delete account. Your songs, your setlists and your data are deleted and cannot be recovered. If you signed in with Apple, the permission you gave Atril is revoked as well.

Cancelling the subscription and deleting the account are two different things: deleting your account does not cancel the subscription, which is done from your device's Settings.
